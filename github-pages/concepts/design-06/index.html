01 / The treatment

Small areas.
Personal priorities.

Electrolysis treats individual hair follicles using a fine probe and a controlled electrical current. Your plan begins with the hair you want to address.

The science, simply

At the follicle.
Beyond the surface.

A fine probe follows the follicle’s natural opening. A small electrical current treats the cells responsible for hair growth, and the treated hair is removed.

Because treatment does not rely on hair pigment, electrolysis can address light and dark hair across skin tones. A series of visits makes room for hair growth cycles.

Three methods. One individual plan.+
Galvanic
Uses a chemical reaction in the follicle.
Thermolysis
Uses heat to treat the follicle.
Blend
Combines galvanic current and thermolysis.

Nav will discuss the approach and settings appropriate to your treatment.

Before you decide

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little understanding can make the first step feel more familiar.

Can light or gray hair be treated?+

Yes. Electrolysis does not depend on pigment, so it can treat blonde, red, gray, and white hairs as well as dark hair. It is used across skin tones; your individual suitability is assessed at consultation.

How many appointments will I need?+

Expect a series, with a schedule tailored to you. Hair grows in cycles, so the hair visible today is only part of the picture. The area, hair density, previous removal methods, and hormonal influences all affect the course of treatment. A fixed completion date cannot be promised.

What does a treatment feel like?+

You may feel brief warmth, stinging, or a pinch. Sensitivity varies by person and treatment area. A small test treatment, when appropriate, can help you understand the sensation and discuss comfort before planning longer appointments.

What if my hair growth is hormonal?+

Electrolysis can treat existing unwanted hair, including hair associated with PCOS or menopause. It does not diagnose or treat hormonal conditions, and hormonal changes can influence new growth. Bring medical questions to a qualified healthcare professional.

How are appointments priced?+

15 minutes: $65; 30 minutes: $95; 45 minutes: $135; 60 minutes: $175; 90 minutes: $265; 120 minutes: $350. All prices are in USD. Session length and your course of treatment determine the overall cost. Discuss recommended appointment lengths and scheduling at your consultation before committing to a plan.
