05 / A little clarity

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Understanding the process is part of feeling ready.

How does electrolysis remove hair?+

A fine probe follows the natural opening of an individual hair follicle. A small electrical current treats the hair-growth cells. Galvanic uses a chemical reaction, thermolysis uses heat, and blend combines the two. The method and settings are selected for the hair, skin, and area.

Does my hair color or skin tone matter?+

Electrolysis does not depend on hair pigment. It can treat every natural hair color, including blonde, red, gray, and white, across skin tones. Your consultation is the place to discuss your skin, hair, and individual suitability.

Why is more than one visit needed?+

Hair grows in cycles, and not every hair is ready to treat at the same time. The amount and structure of hair, previous removal methods, hormones, and appointment consistency affect the course of treatment. Your schedule is reviewed as you go; there is no single timeline for everyone.

What does a treatment feel like?+

You may feel brief heat, a pinch, or a sting. Sensitivity varies with the person and the area. Discuss comfort with Nav; a small test treatment, when appropriate, can help you understand the sensation before a longer session.

What should I know before and after a visit?+

Ask Nav how much visible growth to leave, pause tweezing, waxing, and threading, and arrive with clean skin. Discuss medications and recent skin treatments at your consultation. Temporary redness or tenderness can follow treatment. Follow your individual aftercare guidance and seek medical advice for severe or unexpected symptoms.

Can we discuss hair associated with hormonal changes?+

Yes. Visible hair associated with hormonal changes, including PCOS or menopause, can be part of your treatment discussion. Electrolysis removes hair; it does not diagnose or treat the underlying condition. Medical concerns belong with your healthcare professional.
