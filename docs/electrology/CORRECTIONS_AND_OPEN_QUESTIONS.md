## Source-access limits and how to resolve them

Some Legislative Information endpoints returned access errors, and some large PDFs timed out in the web reader. The reports identify where official Board compilations, government summaries, primary indexed records, or abstract-only research were used. The parent independently downloaded the November 16 Board PDF and read the entity transition and pending-rule status. Those limitations do not justify substituting a blog for the law or claiming full-text review where only an abstract was available.

For an actual legal filing or consequential policy, retrieve the then-operative official text and exact application. For an individualized device question, retrieve the actual model's instructions. For a clinical statement, seek evidence suited to the population and claim. Record the result and update this register instead of silently treating the current snapshot as permanent.
